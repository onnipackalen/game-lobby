    Info

    1. Choose a stake and set out from Cairo for Cape Town.
    2. Every city has a rock. Pick a road, and the rock is turned when you arrive.
    3. Under it is a gem that multiplies your run (Quartz ×1.1, Topaz ×1.25, Emerald ×1.5, Ruby ×2, Sapphire ×3) or a thief. Robbers take everything and end the run; pickpockets steal half.
    4. From every city there is always at least one road toward Cape Town with no robbers on it, only the odd pickpocket. You won't know which road it is, so there's always a way through. If you're robbed, the safe road you missed is shown.
    5. The terrain tells you what to expect: trade roads hold smaller gems, while jungle, wild lands and the Diamond Mines hold bigger gems and more thieves.
    6. Three Stars of Africa hide on every board. Find one and you win on the spot: your run ×2, plus 0.25 for every city you explored first.
    7. You have 10 days of water, and every move uses a day. The caravan always keeps enough to reach Cape Town, so roads that would strand you aren't offered.
    8. Five gold-ringed cities are landmarks: the Oracle counts the Stars north and south, the Guide protects you from the next thief, the Springs add water, the Spice Market banks money and the Diamond Mines hold the richest rocks.
    9. Cash out any time. Reach Cape Town, or run out of water, and the run cashes out by itself.
