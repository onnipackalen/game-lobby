Game info

Sail the route of Magellan's fleet, the first voyage around the world; the checkpoints on the map mark where the fleet really put in. One voyage at a time, and every player sees the same one at the same moment. Join while the ship boards, then cash out before she is lost. The multiplier grows with the distance sailed round the world: make it all the way home and everyone still aboard is paid ×1000.

Storms, trade winds and rough water speed the climb up. They never change where a voyage ends.

Payback99% for any cash-out target
Biggest win×1000 your stake
Stakefrom 1 gold, up to all you have
Betsone, or add a second under your stake (each with its own stake and cash-out)
Voyages lost at the quayabout 2 in 100 (×1.00)

Demo build. Each voyage's result comes from a public schedule: SHA-256 of magellan-live:v1:<hour>:<voyage>; the first 13 hex digits give u, and the ship is lost at ⌊99 ÷ (1 − u)⌋ ÷ 100. Anyone can check a finished voyage by tapping it in the strip. A real-money version would keep the seeds on a server (a hash chain) so no one can see a result ahead of time.
