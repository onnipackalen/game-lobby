How it plays
  • Each spin fills a 7×7 pit with 49 marbles: they ride up the glass lift at the side, out of the top and back down their own glass tube into their column. The lift then shows the next marbles in line: when marbles pop, those are exactly the ones that roll in to fill the gaps.
  • 7 or more touching marbles of one colour (up, down, left, right) pop and pay. Bigger clusters pay a lot more.
  • Ladder Climb: new marbles fall into the gaps, and every pop after the first climbs a rung on the ladder: ×1, ×2, ×3, then ×5 for every pop after that, all in the same spin.
  • Gold marbles (the ones in sunglasses) never pop. Finish a spin with 3 on the grid to win Ladder Spins: 10 free spins, 11 with 4 gold, 12 with 5 or more.
  • In Ladder Spins the ladder never resets. It grows to 13 rungs (×1, ×2, ×3, ×4, ×5, ×6, ×8, ×10, ×12, ×15, ×20, ×25, ×50): every pop pays at the rung your marble is on, then climbs one, and it stays up there for the rest of the bonus. Every gold marble that lands climbs a rung too, and 3 gold in a free spin win 3 more spins.
  • Reach the top rung and the Marble Run opens when the spins are over: tap the peg wall to drop 5 marbles into 9 slots. Each slot hides a multiplier card from ×1 to ×100, drawn before your first drop, so every spot pays the same on average. The cards flip once every marble has landed.
  • Bonus ×2 makes Ladder Spins come up twice as often. Each spin then costs 1.2× your bet; wins still pay on your bet.
  • Buy bonus starts Ladder Spins right away: 12 free spins for 50× your bet, or start higher up the ladder for 100× or 200×.
  • Your lucky marble picks your perk. Each colour marble makes clusters of its colour pay more, and rarer colours get bigger boosts. The Gold star makes the ladder climb faster, and in Ladder Spins its rungs are richer from ×5 up. Every choice is worth about the same over time.
