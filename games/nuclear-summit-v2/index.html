Paytable & rules
  • Clusters pay: 5 or more of the same symbol touching up, down, left or right. Bigger clusters pay more. Pays shown are at your current bet.
  • Winning clusters blow away and new symbols tumble in. Tumbles repeat until there are no new wins.
  • 3 or more Nukes anywhere trigger 10 free spins. In the base game Nukes don't explode.
  • Fallout squares: in free spins Nukes keep dropping in. Every Nuke that lands explodes and leaves a fallout multiplier on the squares it hits: ×2 on the first hit, then it doubles each time a blast hits that square again (×4, ×8, ×16 …), up to ×128. Squares keep their multiplier until the free spins end.
  • Nuke types in free spins: a Suitcase Nuke hits only its own square, a Nuke hits its own square and the 4 touching it, a Tsar Bomba hits every square up to 2 steps away, and a Doomsday doubles every fallout square on the board.
  • Build-up: Nukes drop more often in the first 5 free spins. Doomsday can only land after that, so the squares you light early are the ones it doubles.
  • A winning cluster in free spins is multiplied by the total of the fallout multipliers on the squares it covers.
  • 5 or more Nukes in one free spin add 5 more free spins. Free spins play automatically, one after another.
  • Launch codes: buy a guaranteed trigger (at least 3 Nukes land, then 10 free spins with fewer Nukes than a natural trigger) for 100× the bet. Only in markets that allow feature buys.
  • Maximum win 5,000× the bet per round. Play credits have no cash value.
